my research is primarily concerned with improving health communication and advancing knowledge about persuasive communication.

i have summarized my broad research direction in this research statement, and also provide brief overviews of my active research strands below.

  • how do health departments talk about cannabis?
    • i archived 140+ videos made by american health departments on cannabis, and conducted a rhetorical content analysis
    • this work was conceptualized and conducted by me independently, with funding from a summer research grant from temple's graduate school to cover research expenses
    • one manuscript currently under review for publication
  • how do "citizen journalists" portray the tranq epidemic in philadelphia?
    • my doctoral committee's research group drove a content analysis of how stigma operates in youtube videos capturing folks living with substance use disorder.
    • my role was building the dataset and assisting with manuscript writing and editing
    • manuscript accepted for publication to stigma & health in 2026
  • how do policymakers discuss gun violence?
    • as a methodologist at children's hospital of philadelphia, i helped conduct a rhetorical content analysis of hundreds of tweets and analyze the findings.
    • manuscript published in jmir formative research

  • as part of my doctoral work, i am collecting focus group data and working towards an action research approach towards creating and testing new communication campaigns
    • as ongoing work, i am still in data collection and analysis and aim to have a manuscript under review in mid-2027
  • i also have conducted a great deal of qualitative research as an embedded mixed methods specialist at chop to advance efforts across a broad range of health issues, including:
    • how adolescents in chronic kidney disease thnk about physical activity, published in kidney medicine
    • how services are delivered to pregnant and parenting people with substance use disorder, published in substance abuse treatment, prevention, and policy
    • how young people with idiopathic arthritis deal with medication adherence, published in the journal of pediatrics

  • my doctoral work is built on a community-based participatory research (cbpr) approach because of my conviction that only the target group itself can best define its needs
    • as ongoing work, i am still in data collection and analysis and aim to have a manuscript under review in mid-2027
  • how do we think about dehumanization in communication, and how can we incorporate this understanding into how we teach?
    • as part of a mentored project, i independently wrote a manuscript published in teaching journalism & mass communication
  • additionally, as part of my methods specialist role at chop i worked on the following related projects:
    • how do pediatric rheumatologists think about delivering resilience programming to their patients, published in arthritis care & research
    • how do minoritized medical students experience residency placement for plastic surgery, published in journal of surgical research
